      05 — Questions

      Before you connect.

      What exactly am I looking at?

      One board for the NSE F&O universe: the order blocks price reacted from, the rally strength of each symbol, and the liquidity grade of the strikes around the money. Everything is derived from price, volume and option-chain depth — nothing is copied from a chat room.

      Does it place trades or give calls?

      No. It is a market-analytics board. It shows where institutional activity is building and how deep the option market is; the decision — and the order — stays with you in your broker terminal.

      When is it live?

      Through the NSE session, 09:15 to 15:30 IST. Outside those hours the board holds the last session's read, so weekend preparation looks the same as the close.

      Do I need a broker account?

      For live data, yes — an Upstox API app, connected once. Until then the site runs a sample session so you can see how the engine behaves.

      Does it work on a phone?

      It is a browser board: phone, tablet or desktop, nothing to install. The chart and the picture adapt to the screen you are holding.
